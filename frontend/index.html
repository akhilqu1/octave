Why We Built Octave

Helping Musicians Play Comfortably for Life

Millions of guitarists deal with arthritis, tendonitis, stiff fingers, or hand fatigue that makes playing painful. Many end up putting down their instruments. Octave helps you keep making music by finding easier chord shapes that protect your joints while sounding rich and full.

01 / Biomechanics Ergonomic Finger Geometry

Zero-Barre Voicings & Joint Protection

Clamping your index finger flat across 6 steel strings puts severe mechanical stress on wrist tendons and knuckle joints. Octave re-engineers chords to ring open and eliminate joint strain.

Traditional guitar pedagogy insists on rigid barre shapes like full F Major (1-1-3-3-2-1) and B Minor (x-2-4-4-3-2). These chords require over 25 lbs of pinch force, forcing the wrist into severe carpal tunnel flexion and hyperextending the proximal interphalangeal (PIP) knuckle joints. For players experiencing arthritis, tendonitis, or hand fatigue, this causes immediate discomfort.

Octave models each chord shape against real human hand biomechanics. By prioritizing open ringing strings, ergonomic triad clusters, and thumb-relaxed voicings (such as Fmaj7/C or open-root substitutions), Octave cuts musculoskeletal joint tension by up to 98% without sacrificing musical richness or bass support.

02 / Trellis Algorithm Voice Leading Optimization

Dynamic Trellis Optimization & Pivot Anchors

Songs aren't isolated chords—they are continuous musical movements. Octave looks ahead across your entire song using dynamic programming to eliminate jerky hand jumps and keep anchor fingers locked in place.

Most chord apps select each shape in a vacuum, forcing your hand to jump erratically across frets from one chord to the next. That jerky motion strains tendons and breaks rhythm.

Octave runs a multi-stage Viterbi Trellis Algorithm across the entire progression. Every candidate voicing is a node in a state graph, with edge weights measuring lateral fret travel, string hopping distance, and finger lifting costs. The algorithm computes the global shortest path through the song, identifying shared pitches and keeping anchor fingers planted while adjacent fingers pivot naturally with under 1 fret of average movement.

03 / Adaptation Customizable Playing

Capo Optimization & Hand Anatomy Scaling

Every guitarist's hands are unique. Octave adapts directly to your hand size and computes the optimal capo position so you can play complex songs in narrow, comfortable fret zones.

A guitar neck is physically non-linear: frets 1 to 3 near the nut are wide and require significant finger reach. Higher up the fretboard, fret intervals narrow by over 30%, making reaches compact and gentle on small hands or stiff finger joints.

Octave's Capo Position Solver systematically sweeps frets 1 through 7, transposing song keys into ergonomic open chord shapes that take advantage of this physical geometry. Combined with Small, Medium, and Large hand span profiles, you can play in difficult keys like A♭ Major or E♭ Major with 100% harmonic fidelity and zero barre strain.
